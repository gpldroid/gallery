Beginning again rarely looks like a beginning. More often, it looks like making breakfast after a difficult night, answering one message, or walking a familiar route with a slightly different thought in mind.

Theo March gathers these small acts into a generous meditation on change. The essays are practical without becoming prescriptive, hopeful without pretending that every ending is easy.

The Art of Beginning Again is a companion for uncertain seasons, offering language for the moments when the next step is not yet clear.
