After her grandmother's death, June discovers a drawer filled with letters addressed to people whose names she does not recognise. Each letter reveals a small kindness, an old misunderstanding, or a life that unfolded just beyond the edge of the family story.

Told through correspondence spanning three decades, Letters from the Blue House is a warm, intimate novel about the things we say, the things we keep, and the surprising ways a family learns to tell the truth.

**A note from the reading room**

A lovely choice for readers who enjoy intimate storytelling, found histories, and the emotional architecture of letters.
