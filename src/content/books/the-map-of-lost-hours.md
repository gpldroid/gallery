When the old cinema is scheduled for demolition, Ana returns to the town she left behind. In the attic of her childhood home, she finds a hand-drawn map marked with places she and her brother once promised never to forget.

The map sends her through familiar streets and forgotten rooms, towards the parts of her story she has spent years avoiding.

Mira Sol writes with tenderness about the distance between who we were and who we become. The Map of Lost Hours is a novel about family, the strange geography of memory, and the possibility of finding a way back without becoming the person who left.

**A note from the reading room**

For readers who love quiet mysteries, layered characters, and stories where the landscape feels like another voice in the room.
